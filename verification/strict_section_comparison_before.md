# Strict Section-by-Section Comparison Before Second Pass

The source-of-truth active host page is the predeploy `/host` screen after starting the session. The current dev page at session `93` renders successfully but still differs from the reference in density, rail proportions, and several section-level design decisions.

| Section | Reference behavior | Current dev behavior | Required second-pass direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | Compact chrome with title, active pill, facilitator, time/AI metrics, raised-hand chip, and tight right-side actions. | Header is functional but more spacious and includes larger visual chips; the rhythm is less dense. | Preserve existing actions but reduce visual bulk where possible through tighter spacing and smaller header controls. |
| Left participants rail | Flush far-left panel, compact width, no double inset, compact header, response bar, compact search, dense participant rows, composer at bottom. | Rail is improved but still too wide and airy compared with the reference; search and composer occupy more vertical space, and participant row is larger than reference density. | Make the rail more compact on desktop, reduce internal padding, use smaller row/avatar sizing, and keep the composer anchored without excessive blank feel. |
| Center video room | Dominant media tile with narrow gutters, strong rounded dark media surface, compact controls and thumbnails attached to the media area. | Center tile is dominant, but page gutters and side-rail widths reduce the reference-like cockpit density; thumbnail cards are comparatively roomy. | Prioritize center space by narrowing desktop side rails and gutters, and keep video controls compact. |
| Right control rail | Flush far-right panel, compact tabs, cards stacked densely, subtle tinted backgrounds, small action buttons. | Right rail uses the simplified structure but top card typography and spacing are larger and more spacious than the reference. | Reduce card padding, heading scale, and inter-card gaps while preserving readability and simplified controls. |
| Typography | Dense UI typography, semibold labels, muted metadata, small uppercase section labels. | Some headings are oversized for a control rail; metadata and search are larger than reference. | Use smaller control-panel headings and tighter metadata hierarchy. |
| Spacing | Narrow gutters and compact internal padding. | Desktop layout uses large gaps and wide rails, giving a less cockpit-like feel. | Tighten the workspace grid and panel padding for desktop; retain breathable stacking on smaller widths. |
| Borders and radius | Light borders, soft but not oversized radii; media tile has strong rounded corners. | Large rounded rail/card surfaces are present in places. | Slightly reduce card/rail radius and standardize border treatment. |
| Responsiveness | Fixed compact side rails with flexible center on desktop. | Side rails are relatively wide, limiting the center and making the left rail feel less like the reference. | Use compact fixed side rail widths on large screens and stacked layout at smaller breakpoints. |

Implementation priorities for the strict pass are to narrow and densify both side rails, tighten the full workspace grid, reduce right-card typography and padding, compact the left participant rows and composer, and ensure the center media tile keeps the largest share of the viewport.
